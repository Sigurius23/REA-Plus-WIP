# Investigation methods

## Pick consequential questions

Start with a working map: player situation, ordinary expectation, suspected hidden rule, changed experience, possible business incentive, likely source/consumer and the next observation that would distinguish explanations. Refresh it when a model, service, counter, segment or experiment opens another thread. Use the [examples](inspiration.md) to calibrate which leads deserve depth.

| Thread | Questions and places to follow |
|---|---|
| Gameplay/ad coupling | Does ad availability, value, refusal or completion change generation, success, death or revival? Follow generators, legal-state checks, seeds, ad callbacks and actual grants. |
| Retry/retention | Do starts, losses, return visits or milestones change odds, difficulty, scores or apparent progress? Recover counters, resets, tutorial exclusions and reward schedules. |
| Ad monetization | Do ad-engagement/value segments change rewards, frequency or eligibility? Follow model inputs, thresholds, caps, mediation readiness and reward application. |
| Upsells/pricing | Do failure, hesitation, refusal or spending history change offers? Join catalogs, reference prices, actual store SKUs, currency, histories, holdouts and eligibility. |
| Frontend truthfulness | Does an interface suggest a choice, measured result, scarcity or progress that its hidden rule does not support? Follow outcome timing, display calculations, providers and persistence. |
| Data collection | What is read, serialized, transmitted and used, and when? Include phone model, RAM/storage, identifiers, accessibility/font settings, brightness, networks, SDKs, destinations and consent. |
| Different user versions | Do matching labels conceal different puzzles, moves, rewards, prices or ad routes? Trace traits, experiment arms, feature parameters, assignment inputs and consumers. |
| Server decisions/names | Which requests and returned keys control the experience? Search revealing variable, function, file, trait and arm names, then inspect their use. |
| Lock-in/friction | Are exits, deletion, cancellation, portability or competitors treated differently? Follow navigation, package/deep-link checks, refusal flows, exports and reminders. |

These are starting points. Follow unexpected currency, withheld-reward, social-comparison or notification mechanisms too. Personal data can be a decision input or its own collection/destination/consent consequence. An SDK field alone does not connect that field to first-party gameplay.

## Recover the rule and its economic loop

For a promising lead recover:

1. **Producer:** gameplay/history, OS signal, account, SDK, remote configuration, random experiment or cached default.
2. **Selection:** delivered values, types, thresholds, precedence and overrides.
3. **Decision:** full predicate, arithmetic, units, caps, counter increments, persistence and resets.
4. **Application:** caller/consumer changing a board, eligibility, reward, price, event or displayed text.
5. **Outcome:** operands/results joined to the same action and screen; identify links that remain static or inferred.

Then widen around it: how does the person enter this situation, what follows success/refusal/failure, what encourages another attempt, and what resets the loop? Compare original draws with retries, early gifts with later gifts, base rewards with bonus changes, and relevant opposite states.

Develop the best-guess business/psychological explanation around those changes. “A rising personal-looking number could reassure someone that an upgrade is working” gives an explanation to investigate. Missing backend intent can remain speculative while a recovered client rule is worth discussing.

For profit-focused work, continue to a named commercial outcome: more rewarded-ad views, an earlier top-up, a subscription instead of existing currency, another renewal, or a reduced usable free benefit. Identify the intervening action and its actual offer/reward consumer. More sessions alone leaves a question: which monetized opportunity does the extra session create? Data collection needs its relevant decision/output before it explains higher spending; interesting SDK execution can remain supporting evidence.

Compare with a meaningful alternative rule. For example, if paid coins are consumed before expiring free rewards, calculate what remains after the same viewing sequence under each spending order, then after expiry. This can reveal an earlier need to buy or earn more even without measuring an actual purchase. Label such arithmetic as a conditional scenario, retain the real prices/rewards and material assumptions, and state what a live wallet test must establish. Do not turn an analytic alternative into a claim about a second real app variant.

Choose follow-ups for their chance of closing this chain. More sensor samples do not answer which offer changed; another odds table does not answer whether a paid route becomes more attractive. Keep the strongest plausible motive and its basis visible while tracing the actual spending/ad/renewal route. A revenue-uplift experiment or proof of intent is a separate claim, not a prerequisite for a useful motive hypothesis.

Inspect relevant disclosure screens/copy to understand the interpretation. Record their scope in the ledger; the target remains what code/traffic reveals beyond ordinary use.

## Choose the next decisive comparison

State the unanswered question before creating profiles or repeating actions. Choose state dimensions and sample size for that question.

| Lead | Useful comparison and missing link |
|---|---|
| Retry alters difficulty | Capture starts/losses/quits, values around the threshold and resets. Compare generator outputs; investigate meaningful difficulty/win effects separately. |
| Ads alter the next opportunity | Compare available, offered, declined and completed states with relevant history controlled. Join acknowledgement, counter, generation and grant. Distinguish retry effects from ad-exclusive effects. |
| Same level, different people | Compare normalized boards, objectives, moves and generation rules on the same build/label. Follow differing assignments into those definitions. |
| Refusal alters an offer | Pair back-out/return requests with returned offers and final displayed/store prices. Determine whether a counter is logged or used in a decision. |
| Server overrides a preference | Follow a real off choice through a naturally delivered response, persisted state and later consequential request. Label synthetic branch replays separately. |
| Model/segment changes treatment | Identify eligible states, observed inputs/output and reached consumer. Compare with a meaningful fallback or opposite state. |

Ten identical fresh guests may miss a payer, returner, repeat-failure or high-ad-engagement branch. Preserve a baseline and relevant histories. Exploratory samples establish differences; random outputs need enough observations for the claim. Apply [evidence checks](evidence.md) before turning weights into win rates or correlations into targeting.

## Requests, responses and revealing names

Capture normal traffic around startup, retry, ad offers/completion, shop/paywall visits and refusal. Pair requests/responses with triggering actions. Record endpoint/method or RPC operation, context/time, key paths, values/types and source consumer. Preserve compressed/binary originals and decoding steps.

Distinguish observed responses from defaults, caches, projections and replays. Follow assembly → parsing → assignment/guards → consumer → UI/outcome. Compare changed inputs, returned rules and resulting experiences. Inspect separate fields for real price, reference price, discount label, scarcity, timers, ad frequency, rewards and difficulty.

Search naming variants while retaining recovered spelling. Charles's hypothetical examples `adharvest` and `display80perecentdiscount` are inspiration, not app identifiers. An evocative name should open callers, neighbors, assignments and active values. Preserve a source-linked inventory of useful names/fields, apparent meaning, reached effect, best-guess motive and next test. Follow [capture rules](platform-auth.md) for private originals and sanitized excerpts.

## Traits, arms and versions

Establish the schema: a trait may be an attribute/group label; an arm may select an experiment alternative. Several experiments can overlap. Keep distinct:

- Catalog counts: traits, configurable features and named arms, including inactive entries.
- Assignment combinations delivered to tested identities.
- Effective rules/content after tracing meaningful differences.
- Worldwide totals, requiring separate deployment evidence.

Compare matched builds, capture times, progression and history. Save an anonymized profile matrix and JSON/CSV rule diffs. Check consumers before discarding IDs/seeds as metadata. Show what differs for players and whether displayed labels/statistics change too. Explain observed minima and unknown global totals. Multiplying independent switches does not count real people or deployed versions.

## Lead queue, blockers and completion

Maintain each lead's question, factual basis, story potential, evidence state, missing link and next action. States can be open, static-chain-established, runtime-established, contradicted, inaccessible or expected/low-value. Each iteration should resolve a question, strengthen a connection or open a consequential thread.

Identify whether a blocker is login, account history, store licensing, integrity, feature assignment, ad inventory, missing consumer or inaccessible backend logic. Resolve authorized dependencies early; continue other promising leads independently. Keep web findings scoped to the web. An accessible visible default does not replace the hidden-mechanism investigation.

Before finishing, revisit the strongest story's unanswered links and useful accessible follow-ups. Carry worthwhile hypotheses into the handoff with their basis and next test. Asset volume and formatting checks do not resolve a research question.

Use actual app artifacts/captures for findings. Testimonials, allegations, patents, another app's code and reference videos do not establish this app's behavior. Official documentation and package provenance can support methods; source background/revenue narration separately.
