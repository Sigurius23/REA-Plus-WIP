# Evidence, disclosure and experiments

## Ledger contract

This is the detailed research record, not the reader-facing report template. Preserve the full evidence here; use the short player-focused format in [report output](output.md) for delivery.

Maintain human-readable and machine-readable findings. Each candidate has:

- ID, claim, platform/build and account/session scope.
- App-mechanic explanation, practical user consequence and reproducible trigger.
- Ordinary user expectation, the actual implementation and the surprising difference. When discussing nondisclosure, record the relevant screens/copy inspected and the scope of that claim.
- Best-guess profit/retention motive, proposed psychological mechanism, factual basis, alternative explanations and a useful test. Mark these as hypotheses when intent or targeting criteria are inferred.
- Input/producer → delivered rule → gates/calculation → caller/consumer → outcome/UI chain, with each link observed, static, inferred or missing.
- Surrounding loop: how users reach this situation, what follows success/failure/refusal, what encourages another action and what resets. Link connected findings and distinguish hypothesized connections.
- Evidence state: **observed**, **implemented-unobserved**, **correlation**, **hypothesis** or **contradicted**. Confidence is a separate reasoned assessment.
- Exact artifact paths/SHA-256, line ranges or native addresses, operands/types/units, raw/decoded relationships and capture references.
- For API/name leads: paired request/response and triggering action, endpoint or RPC operation, exact identifier/key path and value/type, source location, active assignment, consumer and UI/outcome join. Record redactions/projection steps separately from the private originals.
- Alternatives, counterevidence, missing links and the next useful test.
- For the next experiment: exact unanswered question, relevant account/history dimensions, comparison/control, captured outcome and what would change the explanation. Preserve its link to the working story outline.
- Implementation surprise, potential profit/retention/user consequence, confidence and editorial value, with reasons. A promising hypothesis can be included before all links are established.

Keep raw originals distinct from redacted projections, reformatted data and analyst reconstructions. Decompiled output is not developer source. Preserve warnings and verify important operations against original bytecode/instructions.

## Discriminating experiments

Prefer matched within-account comparisons for retries/ad effects. Record rule revision, actual start/retry/failure/quit count, seed, moves, boosters, inventory, time, account tenure, switches and ad availability. Across identities, control relevant conditions and state remaining differences. Device model/OS/RAM do not establish wealth or willingness to pay.

Use profiles when they answer a specific question; choose count/dimensions accordingly, rather than always creating ten. Small exploratory samples establish observed differences, not global frequencies or a causal classifier. Compare actual normalized behavior/rules before counting variants: different IDs/revisions/seeds/routing values can refer to the same rule. Overlapping feature flags do not enumerate mutually exclusive people; multiplying options invents a global profile count.

For ad claims, join the offered/completed ad to natural acknowledgement, actual reward application and resulting state. Hook installation, SDK presence, local replay and manual callback invocation are not a watched ad. If no ad is available, the comparison is unmeasured. Bot strategy names and input counts are not accepted moves/outcomes.

Separate natural observation from deliberately changed/synthetic tests. Forced flags, argument/result replacement and account/progress/currency edits cannot prove what the stock app did to ordinary users. Label authorized local replays and keep them outside authentic-run counts. Purchases or actions affecting others require the relevant authorization.

## Meaning checks

- Trace the full predicate: a flag may remove only an extra adjustment while preserving a prior/base reward.
- Recover increments, persistence and resets before calling a cap daily, per-session or lifetime.
- Distinguish starts, retries, actual losses, tutorials and latest-level eligibility.
- Separate weights, normalized probabilities, RNG bucket allocation, candidate samples, accepted visible outcomes and win rates. Each requires its own evidence.
- Check RNG bounds, signs, rounding, indexing, units, float operation order and denominators. Do not label idealized fractions as measured floats.
- Map raw IDs before naming a suppressed colour/item.
- Trace tier/model inputs before naming them wealth, willingness to pay or a psychological diagnosis.
- For privacy, distinguish implemented read → observed read → serialization → transmission/destination → downstream use. An ad-SDK font collector does not prove font-based game difficulty.
- For UI statistics, find the join key, population/time window, source and displayed parameter. A shared statistic across variants raises a validity question, not automatic proof of fabrication or causal purchase benefit.

## Develop findings and motive hypotheses

Suspicious names and inactive code can justify deeper investigation. Follow callers and seek a naturally eligible context. If activation or producer criteria remain unavailable, describe the conditional capability, offer reasoned hypotheses about its purpose or assignment, and record the next test. Keep the source observation and the speculative explanation identifiable.

Inspect defaults, holdouts, failure paths and material alternative explanations, including rollout/tutorial copy. Use counterevidence to refine which motive hypothesis best fits the facts, and update the narrative as the investigation develops. Speculative explanations belong in the report and handoff even when the implementation or backend criteria cannot be fully recovered.
