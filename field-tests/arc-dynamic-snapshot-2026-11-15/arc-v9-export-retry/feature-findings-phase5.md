## Actual reducer coverage

The [exported analyze_function record](evidence/arc-reducer-v9.retained.evidence.json) owns `0x1012a571c..0x1012a7e73`: **10,072 contiguous bytes, 2,518 instructions, 139 basic blocks**. The table at `0x1012a7e74`, dispatched at `0x1012a5bc8`, has 28 distinct targets; every signed32-bit word agrees with the earlier independently retained 112-byte table. Each target has an instruction in the owned body, and the decompiler now emits all 28 case labels. The reproducible [coverage check](check-reducer-coverage.mjs) and [receipt](reducer-coverage-observation.json) distinguish this observed Arc result from the source fixture proofs.

The recovery is conditional on a recursively valid active outer value at the exact tag call, stable ABI/import bindings without interposition, valid disjoint nonwrapping stack allocations and a stable checked post-tag window. Darwin probe effects assume their trusted normal-return model. Pre-tag ownership/copy/nonescape, later lifetime and live reachability are not established. Reflection labels identify ABI tags, with payload cases before empty cases; they are not original source ordering. Native value flow remains truncated at 3,000 operations, and eight synchronous-throw uncertainty sites remain explicitly reported. Table/body completeness therefore does not mean all 28 state effects or the whole app are understood.
